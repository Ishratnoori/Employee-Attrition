# 👥 Employee Attrition Prediction

## 📝 Project Description

Employee attrition refers to the gradual reduction of the workforce in a company due to reasons such as resignation, retirement, or unforeseen events. Understanding and predicting attrition is crucial for HR departments to implement proactive retention strategies and minimize organizational disruption.

This project uses machine learning to identify the key factors influencing attrition and build predictive models that classify whether an employee is likely to leave the organization.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee data to understand trends and patterns related to attrition.
- Discover significant features contributing to employee resignation.
- Build predictive models to classify employees as 'attrition' or 'no attrition'.
- Compare the performance of various ML algorithms on this classification problem.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset includes various employee-related features such as:

- Age
- Job Role
- Monthly Income
- Job Satisfaction
- Work-Life Balance
- Years at Company
- Overtime
- Environment Satisfaction
- And more...

(Data sourced from IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset on Kaggle.)

---

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis

Performed comprehensive EDA to:
- Visualize correlations
- Identify class imbalance
- Explore distributions of numerical and categorical variables
- Analyze patterns in attrition

## 🤖 Machine Learning Models Used

| Algorithm       | Accuracy | Precision | Recall | F1 Score |
|----------------|----------|-----------|--------|----------|
| XGBoost        | 91%      | 92%       | 89%    | 90%      |
| K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) | 89% | 90% | 88% | 89% |
| Decision Tree  | 86%      | 85%       | 84%    | 84%      |
| Support Vector Machine (SVM) | 84% | 100% | 68% | 81% |
| Naive Bayes    | 80%      | 78%       | 82%    | 79%      |

✅ **XGBoost** achieved the best overall performance in terms of accuracy and F1-score.

---

## 🧠 Key Learnings

- Feature importance plays a major role in attrition prediction.
- Overtime, job satisfaction, and environment satisfaction are strong predictors.
- Ensemble models like XGBoost perform better than simple classifiers.
